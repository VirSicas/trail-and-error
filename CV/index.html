Dr Virginia Silió

Research Technical Specialist  ·  UCL Imaging Facility, CCMD  ·  London

Research Technical Specialist and imaging scientist with a PhD in molecular and cell biology and over a decade of experience in core facility management, researcher training, and image analysis. I moved into research infrastructure, building the tools, expertise, and communities that make good science possible.

  • Deliver the UCL's introduction to image analysis course.
  • Deliver microscopy practicals for CELL0016: Advanced Molecular Cell Biology.
  • Develop and maintain open-source bioimage analysis tools on GitHub (bioimage-ml-workflow, cellpose2coordinates — work in progress).

Imaging Technician

Postdoctoral Research Fellow
